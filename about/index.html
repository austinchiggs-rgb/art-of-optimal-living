My story

Two years after completing my undergraduate degree in mechanical engineering, I switched into healthcare and was on track to become a medical doctor.

I’ve always been interested in natural health, but had a huge awakening after discovering the work of Dr. Andrew Kaufman and Dr. Tom Cowan. I quickly realized medical school wasn’t going to give me the tools I needed to help people improve their health, so I left to pursue natural healing. I studied with Dr. Tom Cowan through his New Biology Curriculum and continue to learn under Dr. Andrew Kaufman’s mentorship.

Along the way, I’ve applied natural healing principles to my own health and have completely healed from autoimmune conditions I was told were chronic and lifelong. Now I aim to help people reverse their own health issues. My goal is to make optimal health simple again by spreading the truth and empowering people to take greater ownership of their health.
